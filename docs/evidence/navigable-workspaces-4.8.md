# Navigable Workspaces 4.8 visual evidence

The final release's evidence archive and manifest index fresh desktop (1440 × 1000), portrait touch (390 × 844) and landscape touch (844 × 390) captures from the released source. Retrieve them with the source archive and SHA256SUMS from the [v4.8.0 release](https://github.com/lozknowles/agent-control/releases/tag/v4.8.0).

The evidence covers Estate, device, nested environment, runtime/worker or run, invocation, token/cached-input/output/cost visibility, human-readable history and integrity, reverse historical reconstruction, progressive mobile navigation and capability visibility without activation. The manifest binds each capture to its source and evidence records. Original candidate captures retain their original provenance.

The Pixel demonstration is **historical evidence reconstruction**. It does not assert a fresh physical Pixel execution. Touch browser viewports are labelled as emulation. Missing measurements are not zero, and protected evidence remains subject to existing authorization.

See the [workspace guide](../navigable-workspaces.md), [review](../../NAVIGABLE_WORKSPACES_REVIEW.md) and [release verification](../release-verification-4.8.0.md). Large captures stay outside normal source pulls.
