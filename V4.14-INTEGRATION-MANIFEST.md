## Integration decisions requiring qualification

- The semantic interface and no-progress detector are opt-in APIs of `StructuredChatLoopProvider`; source search has not found production Job admission wiring. They cannot yet be described as ordinary Job capabilities.
- The numbered-display guard is confined to `MutationWorkspace.write`; generic source-write protection across arbitrary harnesses has not been demonstrated.
- The solvability assessor is frozen to five tasks, so the requested generic configurable preflight is not yet established.
- The identity palette has twelve slots and can still collide. Run IDs and text badges remain authoritative; visual colour alone does not identify a Job.
- Multi-file edits are sequential. No transactional or rollback mechanism has been demonstrated.
- The earlier NO_PROGRESS_V1 research run did not causally establish token or wall-time savings, and its frozen corpus is too small to establish a general false-positive rate.

## Release boundary

These files are in a research integration worktree for qualification. Inclusion in a distributable v4.14 package requires individual feature decisions, security and physical gates, clean installation, upgrade, rollback and a final claim audit. No release action is authorised by this manifest.
