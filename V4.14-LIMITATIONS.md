# v4.14 candidate limitations

- This is an experimental integration candidate, not an approved public release.
- Native semantic tools and no-progress handling are opt-in research-loop APIs, not demonstrated as normal governed Job admission options.
- The numbered-output guard is scoped to the disposable mutation workspace. It does not prove protection for every source-write adapter, patch mechanism or external harness.
- The safe-repair corpus is bounded. No broad real-world false-repair rate is established.
- The five-task solvability assessor is not yet a generic configurable preflight for arbitrary workloads.
- NO_PROGRESS_V1 has 18 frozen cases, mostly synthetic, and one benchmark-native physical HR control that ended 0/5. Its observed token and time difference is not a causal saving because the trajectories diverged before intervention.
- The detector observes completed tool interactions; slow active generation, unique unproductive tool wandering and hidden side effects are outside its direct scope.
- Semantic diagnostics and forensic response capture have not passed a new integrated, permission-governed physical evaluation.
- Multi-file edits execute sequentially. Atomicity and rollback of a partially completed batch are not established.
- The twelve-colour Run identity palette can collide. Text Run IDs remain authoritative; colour alone is never the sole identifier.
- The 18.13-second Job identity clip shows three concurrent local read/model/test Jobs, not the requested full integrated v4.14 release-candidate scenario.
- AutomationBench Agent Control attempts remain zero; no public/private benchmark comparison or Strands cost claim is made.
- v4.13.0 diagnostic coverage remains PARTIAL; the held-out classifier evaluation remains 47 AI-reviewed cases. Pixel physical identity remains unverified.
- Live video is genuine browser capture in the bounded demonstration and is not cryptographically signed.
- No v4.13-to-v4.14 upgrade, rollback, fresh install, multi-host physical qualification, multi-model release qualification, or soak claim has passed yet.
